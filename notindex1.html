About this test

This is a self-report questionnaire that scores three things: how extroverted you are, how neurotic (emotionally reactive) you are, and a lie scale that flags overly socially-desirable answering.

  • Answer with your first instinct, not after long deliberation — there are no right or wrong answers.
  • Answer every question honestly; don't skip any.
  • Extroversion and Neuroticism are each scored out of 24. The Lie score is out of 9 — a score of 5 or higher means the result should not be taken too seriously, since it suggests you may be answering in a way that makes you look good rather than answering candidly.
  • Your E and N scores are plotted on a circle to show which of the four classic temperaments — Sanguine, Choleric, Phlegmatic, Melancholic — you sit closest to. The further from the center, the more pronounced the trait.
  • This is a simple, dated instrument, not a clinical diagnosis. If your result doesn't match how you actually see yourself, trust yourself over the test.
